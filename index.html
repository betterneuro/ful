Before you join us: a short note everyone agrees to
  1. You know your body best. Go at your own pace, skip or stop anything, and tell the facilitator about any health condition, injury or pregnancy that matters.
  2. This isn't medical care. Our events support well-being but don't replace a licensed professional.
  3. Facilitators lead their own events and are responsible for what happens in them.
  4. You'll help keep the space safe: no alcohol or other substances, and follow the house agreements.
  5. You take part by choice and accept that gatherings carry some risk, like strain, dizziness, falls or strong emotions.

The important part: To the fullest extent the law allows, you release the facilitator, Better Neuro Solutions and its team, and the Ful community from any claims for injury, illness, loss or damage that come from your participation, except for gross negligence or intentional misconduct.

House agreements
 · tap to read
        
Every renter agrees to these when booking. They keep the space safe, peaceful and ready for the next group.

        

          

            
Your time

            
    
              
  • Every booking includes 30 minutes before your start time to arrive and set up, at no extra charge.
    • 
              
  • Please close your event and be out of the space within 15 minutes after your end time. Need more setup or breakdown time? Ask when you book and we'll arrange it.
    • 
              
  • A Better Neuro Solutions team member opens and locks up the space.
    • 
            

          

          

            
Safety

            
    
              
  • No more than 30 people, including facilitators and children.
    • 
              
  • No open flames. LED candles are welcome.
    • 
              
  • Keep doorways and exits clear at all times.
    • 
            

          

          

            
Care of the space

            
    
              
  • No alcohol or other substances on the property.
    • 
              
  • No tape, nails, tacks, glitter or confetti. Ask before hanging anything.
    • 
              
  • Put furniture, floor beds and pillows back where you found them.
    • 
              
  • Take your trash and leftover food with you, wipe surfaces, and empty the refrigerator.
    • 
              
  • If the space needs extra cleaning afterward, a $75 cleaning fee applies.
    • 
            

          

          

            
Responsibility

            
    
              
  • The renter is responsible for guests' behavior and for any damage or missing items.
    • 
              
  • Keep music and voices at a level that respects neighbors, and wrap up loud activity by 10pm.
    • 
              
  • Service animals are welcome. Other pets need approval in advance.
    • 
              
  • Every event, free or paid, needs a signed Space Use & Facilitator Agreement before the date is confirmed.
